Six bell tones made from a few sine partials: an app opening from the Dock, a drop, the Trash, a minimise, a timer or focus session ending, and a theme being applied. Three soft key sounds, a space and a return for Typing Sounds.

Choose it in Dock › Sounds or in Typing Sounds; turn on **Play the pack's key sounds** to type with it. Built-in stays the default.

A small example of a sound pack. Every sound was synthesised for it.
