Atelier's own Starfield shader in deep blues, a large flip clock with the date, the weather in the lower left and your next three events in the lower right.

A scene carries no code: it names a background and the layers drawn on top of it. Install it, then choose Use in Screen Saver in Mods › Library, or pick it in the Screen Saver place. The weather and your events come from what Atelier writes for the screen saver; the saver itself only reads.
