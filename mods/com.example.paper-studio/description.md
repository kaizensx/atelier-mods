# Paper Studio

Warm paper and serif type, and nothing that moves.

- **Wallpaper**: a still mesh in dusk colours, no animation at all.
- **Dock**: frosted, with a bar indicator and no icon shadows.
- **Widgets**: frosted amber, serif type, soft corners.
- **Clock**: a serif face on the desktop.

Made for long days of reading and writing, and kind to the battery.
