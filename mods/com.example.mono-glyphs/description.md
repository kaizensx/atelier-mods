Twelve icons drawn for the Atelier Dock, Orbit, the Launcher and Command: Safari, Finder, Mail, Notes, TextEdit, Terminal, System Settings, the App Store, folders, Downloads and the Trash, empty and full.

Choose **Use** on its page. Click any icon in the preview to keep that app's own icon. Nothing outside Atelier changes: real apps and Apple's Dock keep their icons.

A small example of an icon pack. The format is in Atelier's docs/PACKS.md.
