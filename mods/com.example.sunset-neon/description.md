# Sunset Neon

A neon grid under a striped sun.

- **Wallpaper**: the *Retro Horizon* live wallpaper.
- **Dock**: neon, with a glow indicator and a wave curve.
- **Widgets**: a pink-to-orange gradient, monospaced type.
- **Clock**: big and bold. Icons tinted pink.
- **Sounds**: a little funk when it is applied.

Best on a dark setup, at night.
