# Midnight Glass

Ink-blue glass over a slow nebula.

- **Dock**: glass, dot indicators, soft reflection.
- **Widgets**: glass with an indigo tint.
- **Wallpaper**: the *Nebula* live wallpaper on a custom palette, slowed for the night.
- **Menu bar**: notchless.
- **Icons**: clear, dark.

Applying it changes nothing you can't take back: **Revert** in Looks restores your setup.
